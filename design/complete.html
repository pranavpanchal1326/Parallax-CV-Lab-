The fault thread · ISS-0417 on C1-CAM

One fault, every view.

A camera fault reported at a capture station reaches every screen that depends on that camera, and leaves only when someone has verified the repair.

  1. Student

    Reports the camera interruption at the station.

  2. Mobile

    An offline report stays visibly pending. It never says “sent”.

  3. Technician

    Classifies the symptom, restricts only C1‑CAM and previews the effect on bookings.

  4. Faculty

    Sees that the C1 rotation is now short and chooses how to re-plan.

  5. Public

    The wall display and a hang tag show a notice with no names and no fault detail.

  6. Technician

    Repairs, verifies under the reported conditions, or keeps the issue open.

  7. Everyone

    Release restores bookings B‑2047 and B‑2049. The plan and the bookings agree again.
