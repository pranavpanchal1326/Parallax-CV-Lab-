Part 06 · The student’s journey · P6-A

A question. A traceable conclusion.

A student picks an investigation, books everything it needs, finds the right bench, captures and compares, writes up what the evidence supports, and hands in one named revision. These are the 27 screens that carry them through it, and the 8 that catch them when something breaks.

Captured from the project’s Figma file on 2 October 2026. Every booking, capture and receipt shown is a prepared example. No student has tested this journey yet.
