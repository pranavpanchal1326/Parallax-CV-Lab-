Part 04 · Space and 3D · Geometry P4-D

A place to make the invisible understandable.

A Signal & Computer Vision Lab conceived as a scientific studio: quiet computing along one wall, a darkened bay for controlled capture, a bench for hands-on signal work, and one shared table where a group can argue about what the data means.

01 · Function before form

A clear route. A deliberate place for every task.

  1. A desk you can roll up toCV-04

    The rotated desk leaves a 1.2 × 0.9 m clear floor outside the main route. No chair is placed in that space.

  2. A camera aimed at one spotC1

    2.05 m from camera to the object-stage centre. The backdrop is a separate surface; optical framing remains a calibration task.

  3. Four places to discussCOL

    A 2.4 m table supports four seats at 0.6 m centres; warm ash distinguishes discussion from instrument work.

  4. One line to followSPN

    A 1.5 m route connects both conceptual exits. The suspended installation remains above 2.5 m.
