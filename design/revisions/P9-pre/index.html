Parallax

A living atlas of perception

The whole project, in order

One room, drawn twice: once in space, once on screen.

Parallax is the shift you see when you look at one thing from two positions. This project uses that idea to design a small laboratory where students learn how signals and cameras measure the world, and the software that helps them book it, find it and use it.
