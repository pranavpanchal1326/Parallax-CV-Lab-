Findings

What the comparison showed.

Two of these are fixable with a token change. One is structural and rules a study out.

  • F1Atlas: brand blue fails on dark instrument viewers (2.48, 2.97). Fixable with instrument tokens.
  • F2Manuscript: red accent collides with danger red; selection, detections and the primary CTA read as errors on P06, P14, P31. Structural.
  • F3Theatre: every pair passes; dark UI is a poor fit for booking, long reading and lit-room signage.
  • F4Atlas: success text 4.43:1 — one step darker green reaches 4.99:1.
  • F5All: the shared skeleton carried three characters; identity can live in tokens (de-risks Phase 5).
  • F6Atlas: the isometric cutaway doubles as Lab Map, signage key and 3D blockout reference.

Decision · Direction v1

Atlas, plus what the others did better.

Atlas is the base, with Theatre’s dark instrument surfaces and Manuscript’s editorial serif. These pages are set in it.

Keep

Atlas

Light UI surfaces, ultramarine actions, Familjen Grotesk / DM Sans / JetBrains Mono, isometric spatial identity, radius 4, underline navigation.

Fixed in Atlas+

ok → #1C7148 (4.99:1); instAccent and instWarn as above. Proven on P12 and P14 in section "Direction v1".

Borrow

From Theatre

Dark instrument surfaces; instAccent #6EE7C5 (11.64:1 on instBg), instWarn #F2C14E (10.48:1); field-of-view language for Scene Studio.

From Manuscript

Newsreader italic for editorial moments only (brief intros, case studies, pull quotes); numbered evidence notes.

Reject

Red brand accent; dark theme as global UI; decorative motifs on dense operational screens.
