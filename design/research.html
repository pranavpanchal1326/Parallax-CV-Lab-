Persona hypotheses

Six roles, not six people.

Archetypes of a role, not portraits. Details that would not change a design decision are left out.

PA

primary · hypothesis

The First-Run Experimenter

“I want to know exactly what to bring and what to do first, before I'm at the bench.”

Early in the course; first time on the vision rig; uses a laptop and phone.

Wants, frustrations, design response
Wants
Understand what the experiment is asking; arrive with the right setup; avoid looking lost.
Likely frustrations
Unclear kit list (H01); unfamiliar controls (H06); unsure what evidence counts.
Likely behaviour
Reads the brief late; follows peers; photographs the bench with a phone (H09).
What the design gives them
Preparation checklist (P05), complete-kit booking (P06), setup illustration and readiness check (P11), labelled workbench stages (P12).
Access
May prefer reduced motion; benefits from plain-language labels.
PB

primary · hypothesis

The Advanced Builder

“If I can't reproduce it, it didn't happen.”

Final-year; runs extended investigations; reuses configurations.

Wants, frustrations, design response
Wants
Fast access to precise parameters; reproducible results; publishable evidence.
Likely frustrations
Settings not recorded with captures (H03); equipment unavailable without warning (H04).
What the design gives them
Saved configurations (P12), provenance strip (C39), comparison viewer (P14), stable submission revision (P16), project gallery (P17).
PC

primary staff · hypothesis

The Instructor

“I need to know who is ready before the session, not halfway through it.”

Runs scheduled teaching sessions for a cohort; assesses reports.

Wants, frustrations, design response
Wants
Sessions start on time with ready kits; fair attendance; assessment linked to evidence.
Likely frustrations
No readiness view (H07); paper attendance (H05); reports without settings (H03).
What the design gives them
Faculty workspace with readiness and roster (P27), schedule (P19), evidence-linked rubric (P28).
PD

primary staff · hypothesis

The Lab Technician

“A fault I hear about after the class is a fault I couldn't prevent.”

Maintains equipment, calibration, storage, and returns.

Wants, frustrations, design response
Wants
Know where every asset is; catch faults before they affect classes; complete returns.
Likely frustrations
Verbal fault reports without context (H04); location by memory (H08); missing accessories after sessions.
What the design gives them
Technician console with fault queue and movement history (P25), calibration record (P26), return check (P11 variant), QR passport (P09).
PE

secondary · hypothesis

The Administrator

Wants
Correct roles and resource definitions; defensible capacity reporting.
What the design gives them
Administration (P30), access management flow (W10), audit events.
PF

tertiary · hypothesis

The Visitor

Wants
Orientation and approved demonstrations without private records.
What the design gives them
Wall display (P32), public notices (P22), project gallery public view (P17).
