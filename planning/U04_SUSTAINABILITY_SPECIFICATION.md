# PARALLAX U04 sustainability specification

Designer-reviewed proposal · 3 October 2026 · P4-D assumed 10 × 7 m room. Site, product, circuit and institutional approval are unverified. No measured savings, connected hardware or certification is claimed.

[Printable sheet](../design/U04_SUSTAINABILITY_SHEET.html) · [Running view](http://127.0.0.1:4190/#/environment)

## SUS-01 · Lighting

Zone the light; preserve the experiment

Separate general task-light and capture-light circuits. Use maintainable LED luminaires with replaceable drivers; choose flicker and dimming performance against the camera method. Keep exit and emergency lighting outside standby.

**Physical anchors:** W1, COL, C1, SPN. **Owner:** Facilities electrical lead + teaching technician.

**Trade-off:** W1 daylight can support COL work; C1 needs repeatable illumination. Use an adjustable glare screen at W1 and capture-zone shielding without blocking ventilation or circulation.

**Evidence required:** Survey task-plane illuminance and glare in daylight/evening; record luminaire power, driver serviceability and camera compatibility. Circuit map and facilities approval required.

**Acceptance:** Lighting zones match the task and circuit schedule; repeat capture settings with daylight controlled; document maintenance access and emergency exclusions.

**Status:** Reviewed design proposal · no installed luminaires or lighting survey

## SUS-02 · Natural ventilation

An opening is a starting point

Retain manual access to the assumed south operable W1 (x 4.4–6.2 m). Review occupied-room airflow and outdoor conditions. D1/D2 are exit landmarks, not a proposed ventilation control or guaranteed cross-flow path.

**Physical anchors:** W1, C1, D1, D2. **Owner:** Facilities ventilation lead.

**Trade-off:** One assumed window does not prove cross-ventilation. Drafts, dust and changing light can disrupt C1; facilities must resolve these with the teaching method before operation.

**Evidence required:** Opening/site survey, outdoor-air review, occupied sampling plan and calibrated monitor placement. Window state is unrecorded; CO₂ examples cannot certify safe air.

**Acceptance:** Facilities documents when manual window use is suitable and what to do when it is unsuitable; approved procedures remain available on posted signs.

**Status:** Reviewed concept · airflow, window state and approval unverified

## SUS-03 · Smart power

Review work before reducing load

Map lighting, computing and signal resources to separately reviewed circuits. Protect network, emergency services and any unmapped circuit. Check reservations, acquisition and saved work before a staff-reviewed standby proposal.

**Physical anchors:** C1, CMP, S1. **Owner:** Technician + facilities electrical lead.

**Trade-off:** A free booking slot does not establish an idle or safe-to-switch device. Warm-up, calibration, unsaved work and standby load remain asset-specific checks.

**Evidence required:** Metered load and standby baseline over comparable periods, surveyed circuit IDs, asset operating instructions and approved switching policy. The app has modelled intervals and no power control.

**Acceptance:** Active/restricted/reserved resources are excluded; a technician reviews remaining dependencies; confirmation records a simulation only, with no measured savings claim.

**Status:** Working local preview · hardware and policy approval pending

## SUS-04 · Recyclable furniture

Replace a part, keep the frame

Candidate assembly: mechanically joined metal frame, removable worktop and independently replaceable cable tray/panels. Prefer documented parts and disassembly instructions to permanently bonded mixed materials.

**Physical anchors:** CV-01, CV-04, S1-B. **Owner:** Procurement + technician.

**Trade-off:** Recyclability depends on material separation and a local recovery route. Repair access must retain CV-04 approach space and bench stability; footprint alone does not prove accessibility.

**Evidence required:** Supplier bill of materials, fastener/parts schedule, load/stability information, repair instructions and written local take-back/recovery route; no supplier selected.

**Acceptance:** A damaged panel can be replaced independently; disassembly order and spare parts are documented; a receiver confirms each claimed material recovery route.

**Status:** Reviewed specification proposal · procurement evidence absent

## SUS-05 · Green materials

Make each claim traceable

Compare durable, cleanable candidate worktops, floor finishes and low-emission coatings. Request product-specific emissions, composition, recycled-content and durability evidence; record manufacturer, revision and declared scope.

**Physical anchors:** COL, CMP, C1, SPN. **Owner:** Procurement + facilities materials lead.

**Trade-off:** A green label does not establish cleanability, camera glare, slip resistance or durability. Trial matte C1 surfaces against teaching use and protect the circulation spine from unsuitable finishes.

**Evidence required:** Candidate product evidence, maintenance/cleaning compatibility and local end-of-life route. Verify claims per product; no certified or purchased material is asserted.

**Acceptance:** Claim register links each chosen product and document to its location, owner and review; unsupported claims stay unverified and products remain candidates.

**Status:** Reviewed specification proposal · no certified product selection
