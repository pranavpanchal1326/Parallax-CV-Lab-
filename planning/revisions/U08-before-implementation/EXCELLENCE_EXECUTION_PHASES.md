Authoritative reservations, sensor observations and policy decisions stay simulated/local until their real integration exists.

U02 and U03 can overlap because U03 initially preserves existing designs; U04–U08 introduce new product decisions informed by research. U09 checks start with every increment rather than waiting for the end. U10 assembles and rehearses documents drafted throughout.
