# PARALLAX — U02 research and design synthesis

Revision U02-R1 · 3 October 2026 · public desk research and authored design evidence.

**Delivered:** a reviewed web evidence base, ranked opportunity map, hypothesis review, traceability for all twenty requirements, source-linked wireframes and an illustrated flagship storyboard. **Local participants: zero.** Published research supports a design rationale; it does not turn the project's hypothesis personas into validated personas. The participant-dependent part of U02 remains open.

## Scope and method

PARALLAX supports students conducting signal and computer-vision practicals, faculty supporting learning and technicians maintaining shared equipment. Administrators and visitors receive narrower projections. The immediate horizon is the assignment demonstrator and the next upgrade increments, not deployment of a building-control system.

We reviewed fourteen public sources: five laboratory/education/provenance research sources, two institutional operating pages, three standards/guidance/framework sources, two AI education studies and two public discussion threads. Research questions were preparation, evidence reconstruction, equipment coordination, recovery, access, environmental interpretation, sustainability and AI learning support. Queries combined these topics with physics laboratories, electronic notebooks, university equipment policies, WCAG, ventilation, LEAF and tutoring trials. This was a focused review, not a systematic literature review or a saturation claim.

The [source register](U02_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) records reading depth, dates, locators and limitations. Primary studies and institutional guidance lead the rationale. Public discussions generate questions and supply counterexamples; they do not establish frequency. Search snippets, abstracts and full-text readings are distinguished. The old unspecified utilisation figure is excluded as a design basis. No local interviews, surveys, observations, room measurements or policy approvals were performed.

## Affinity synthesis: six tensions

The following clusters are researcher interpretations across sources, **not themes coded from local interview notes**. Severity is a prospective consequence rating: critical = potential safety/scientific misrepresentation; major = blocked task or lost evidence; moderate = avoidable effort. Frequency is unknown in every row. Confidence concerns the external rationale; local confidence remains untested.

| Priority / cluster | Source basis | Opportunity and design consequence | Prospective severity / external confidence | Local uncertainty and disconfirming evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Finish the task versus defend the evidence | S01–S03, S05 | Keep failed captures, method/source/settings revisions and uncertainty visible. Compare observations before asking for the student's interpretation. | Critical scientific misunderstanding / moderate, relevant physics and provenance contexts | Do students already keep adequate notes? Can they reconstruct a capture from current practice? H03/H12 remain open. |
| 2 · A booking exists versus the whole kit is usable | S06–S07; S13–S14 as questions | Reserve dependencies together, distinguish training from preparation, explain the conflicting item, retain a compatible alternative. | Major blocked practical / moderate operational examples, low anecdotal support | A small lab may function well with a shared calendar and a technician. Confirm local quantities and policy before building more automation. |
| 3 · Recovering work versus believing it is finished | S05–S06, S08 | Separate draft, saved revision, submitted revision and receipt. Preserve state on interruption and give each physical handover an owner. | Major lost work / moderate design rationale, local incidence unknown | Are receipt failures a meaningful local pain or mainly a prototype scenario? Test understanding without teaching the state names. |
| 4 · Looking informed versus making a justified room decision | S09, S06, S10 | Show provenance and age; refuse a composite Safe score. Review energy assumptions with booking and asset-specific policy. | Critical unsupported safety inference / high guidance basis, site application unverified | No sensors, measurements or approved thresholds exist. A screenshot cannot demonstrate ventilation or savings. |
| 5 · Convenient assistance versus independent understanding | S11–S12, S01–S03 | A narrow, source-linked, hint-first assistant; student attempt and unaided follow-up. Manual lookup and scientific reasoning are separate modes. | Critical learning/claim distortion / moderate; mixed contexts and short horizons | No PARALLAX AI interaction has been evaluated. Faster task completion may conceal weaker understanding. |
| 6 · Spatial richness versus usable access | S08; local H09–H11 are unresolved | Typed identifiers, lists and 2D routes accompany scans and spatial views; keyboard and reflow are core paths. | Major access exclusion / high standard basis; physical fit unverified | Verify real access preferences, devices, routes and room circulation. No evidence establishes demand for AR or voice. |

No RICE score is calculated: local reach and effort estimates are unavailable. Priorities reflect consequence, rubric relevance, preserved product commitments and implementation leverage, not measured popularity.

### Empathy prompts, without invented quotations

For PA, ask what they actually **said** before preparing, **did** when missing a part and **expected** from a help message; infer frustration only from a recorded account or observation. For PC, distinguish stated assessment goals from observed support decisions. For PD, capture the real handover chain and workarounds before assuming memory-based tracking. Feelings, quotations and pain frequency remain blank until collection. The historical empathy maps remain explicitly H-labelled.

Frontstage records must have backstage responsibility. Reservation policy belongs to a lab owner; safe release to the authorised technician; learning interpretation to the student with faculty guidance. Environment sampling and shutdown policy require qualified local review. Current role switches preview these responsibilities; they are not authenticated permissions. A future service must define availability while staff are absent, escalation and reconciliation of offline records.

## Opportunity roadmap and bounded decisions

**Evidence package now:** link all twenty provisional requirements to sources, design decisions and remaining tests; preserve twelve wireframe layouts and add research annotations plus three operational boards for readiness, attendance correction and asset movement; illustrate the condition-to-claim-to-receipt story and three recovery branches. Do not change scientific fixtures or silently alter domain behaviour in a documentation increment.

**Next implementation:** source approval workflow and AI evaluation (U06); retained one-variable rehearsal and fault consequences (U05); coverage/parity ledger (U03); ownership/policy contracts (U07). Prioritise the common task and recovery before optional voice, AR/VR or predictive-maintenance claims. Existing dashboards, energy previews and condition replay are implemented examples, not integrated facility evidence.

**Later, conditional:** live devices and any twin require registered asset/observation IDs, sampling and calibration policy, timestamps, stale/offline rules and an owner. Forecasting needs real history plus out-of-sample evaluation. AR guidance requires spatial accuracy and an equivalent static route. Voice needs explicit activation, transcript correction and a quiet fallback. Neither modality is justified by local demand yet. CCTV design needs a purpose, coverage/privacy policy and qualified physical review, not a decorative feed.

### AI experience brief for U06

The two AI studies warrant a testable, cautious product hypothesis, not the claim that AI improves this lab. The [register](U02_SOURCE_REGISTER.md#s11) records the correction/access limitation for S11. This proposed assistant has two explicit modes:

1. **Find an approved manual passage:** show source title, revision, section and why it applies to the named asset/method. Unknown or withdrawn source means no answer; offer the manual index or staff route.
2. **Help me reason:** ask for the student's attempt, give a small hint, point to their retained evidence and invite a next step. Never fabricate a measurement, generate a final assessed claim or equate model confidence with correctness.

Context is opt-in and inspectable: method ID, selected capture IDs and relevant source excerpts. A visible context summary precedes an answer. Cancel retains the student's text; network failure has a retry route; an incorrect citation can be flagged to a named content owner. Faculty approval, server-held credentials and evaluation precede real inference. No provider or live assistant is implemented by U02.

Evaluate correct supported help, abstention, withdrawn revisions, contradictory sources, incomplete capture context, prompt injection in source material, cross-user leakage and fabricated numerical answers. Also evaluate unaided explanation after hints. A pleasant or fast chat is insufficient evidence of learning. Baselines and acceptance thresholds need faculty agreement before any results claim.
