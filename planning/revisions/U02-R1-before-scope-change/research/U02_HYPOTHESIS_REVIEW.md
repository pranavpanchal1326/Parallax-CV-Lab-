# PARALLAX — all twelve hypotheses reviewed

U02-R1 · 3 October 2026. All twelve remain **open local hypotheses**. External relevance is not local confirmation. Earlier high/medium confidence labels were author estimates, not collected evidence.

## H01 · Students discover missing or incompatible kit only at the bench.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis. **Sources:** S04, S06, S07.

Preparation and equipment permissions are meaningful design contexts; none establishes when our students discover problems.

**Validation:** M1/M2 plus T1/T4: recount a recent missing-kit incident, inspect current preparation, explain complete-kit dependencies.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Current manifests and staff checks already prevent missing-kit surprises.

## H02 · Current booking covers room and time, not individual devices and accessories.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis. **Sources:** S06, S07, S13, S14.

External policies and accounts show several equipment-booking practices; the local system remains unknown.

**Validation:** M4/M1 plus T2: collect the actual booking form/policy and walk through a real dependency conflict.

**Counterevidence to seek:** The local system already reserves resources, or station-only booking is appropriate for fixed equipment.

## H03 · Acquisition settings are lost between capture and report writing.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis. **Sources:** S01, S05.

Reasoning and structured provenance justify traceable records; actual loss and its frequency are not established.

**Validation:** M2/M4 plus T5/U-T14: reconstruct a recent capture from existing notes before showing the prototype.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Current instruments and notebooks retain the settings students need without extra work.

## H04 · Faults are reported verbally and do not reach future bookers.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis. **Sources:** S06, S13.

Fault ownership and coordination warrant investigation; neither source describes our reporting chain.

**Validation:** M1 technician/M4 plus T7/T8: trace the latest incident from report to affected booking and verified release.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Existing logbooks and staff handovers already communicate faults reliably.

## H05 · Attendance is taken on paper and corrected informally.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis — no direct secondary support. **Sources:** No direct selected source / coverage.

No selected source establishes the local attendance method. Preserve the requirement as provisional.

**Validation:** M4/M1 faculty: inspect an anonymised attendance workflow and correction policy; test the correction task.

**Counterevidence to seek:** The institution already has a mandatory digital attendance system with an audit trail.

## H06 · Novice students hesitate with unfamiliar instruments and wait for help.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis. **Sources:** S04, S01, S12.

Preparation and scaffolding are plausible responses; waiting behaviour is a local question, not an observed finding.

**Validation:** M2/T1: observe unfamiliar preparation without prompting and distinguish productive thinking from blocked action.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Pauses are deliberate reasoning, and peer/faculty help is already timely and educationally useful.

## H07 · Faculty cannot see cohort readiness before a session starts.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis — indirect context only. **Sources:** S02.

Learning expectations merit explicit investigation; E-CLASS does not demonstrate a missing cohort-readiness view.

**Validation:** M1 faculty/M4: ask about the last readiness decision and the evidence actually used; test a cohort task.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Faculty already get sufficient readiness information; aggregate completion would not improve support.

## H08 · Asset location relies on technician memory.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis — indirect context only. **Sources:** S06, S10.

Equipment responsibility and organisation are relevant contexts; no selected source establishes memory dependence.

**Validation:** M1 technician/M3/M4: locate an unfamiliar asset using current records and trace a recent movement.

**Counterevidence to seek:** A maintained register and labels already resolve location accurately.

## H09 · Students already use phones for reference during experiments.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis — no direct secondary support. **Sources:** No direct selected source / coverage.

Responsive design is a product commitment; it does not prove phone use in this lab. Earlier confidence is not evidence.

**Validation:** M2/M5: observe permitted device use and ask about access preferences, connectivity and phone restrictions.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Phones are prohibited, inconvenient or inaccessible; shared bench displays are the usual reference.

## H10 · Circulation and uncontrolled light disturb camera capture.

**Status:** Open site hypothesis — no direct secondary support. **Sources:** No direct selected source / coverage.

The assumed geometry and illustrative lighting fixture do not establish real room disturbances.

**Validation:** M2/M3: survey routes, light sources and actual capture disturbance with qualified staff; log time/conditions.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Existing separation and controlled lighting already protect capture; another cause explains failures.

## H11 · A 3D map helps orientation more than it helps booking.

**Status:** Open comparative hypothesis. **Sources:** S08.

Accessibility supports equivalent task paths, not a ranking of 3D usefulness.

**Validation:** M7/T3/T7: counterbalance plan/list and 3D tasks; record comprehension, assistance and access barriers.

**Counterevidence to seek:** 3D adds effort or distraction; the list/plan is equally effective for orientation.

## H12 · Students treat model confidence as correctness.

**Status:** Open local hypothesis — indirect context only. **Sources:** S02, S03, S11, S12.

Epistemic expectations and AI-learning evidence justify probing interpretation; none directly tests this model-confidence claim.

**Validation:** M7/T5/U-T14: interpret a high-confidence incorrect illustrative result and explain what evidence establishes correctness.

**Counterevidence to seek:** Students already distinguish model score, calibration and independent ground truth.
