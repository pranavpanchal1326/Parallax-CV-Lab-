# U05 delivery — scientific signature experiences

3 October 2026 · local demonstrator complete · AI remains U06.

U05 completes the local experiences specified in blueprint sections 30.2–30.4. Student reasoning stays separate from experimental evidence. The original prototype, Scene Studio model and geometry remain available. This is designer and technical review under the agreed secondary-research scope; no participant or physical-lab validation is claimed.

| Experience | Delivered behaviour | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| S-EXP-01 · recorded conditions | Select a retained condition and inspect individual differences. Replay shows captured settings, method/fixture/geometry revisions, generated reference, resource state and observation provenance, precise timestamps, reception and connection. Missing values are explicitly not comparable. A selected field asks a bounded interpretation question. | Later environment changes and reload leave recorded context unchanged. Differences establish no causation. Older records gain no invented context. |
| S-EXP-02 · E01 sampling | Prediction → choice of 3, 4, 8 or 20 Hz → matched baseline/candidate waveform plates and sample table → student reflection. The signed alias matches the sampled values; exact 4 Hz Nyquist boundary warns that this zero-phase sine produces zero-crossing samples. | Known generated reference, not device acquisition. Frequency, amplitude, zero phase and duration stay fixed. Noise, timing error and filtering are omitted. |
| S-EXP-02 · E06 lighting | Prediction → frontal/side choice → two authored illustrations → reflection. Same frontal condition explicitly repeats the image. Retained settings are shown without claiming that their effects were calculated. | Qualitative illustration; no irradiance, real lens/exposure response or detector accuracy calculation. |
| S-EXP-02 · Scene Studio geometry | Prediction → bounded horizontal field of view at 18°, 40° or 90° → existing camera image, plan and computed clipping/occlusion interpretation → reflection. Baseline/candidate toggle holds camera distance, offset, height, pan, tilt and objects fixed. Plan-only fallback is explicitly approximate. | The original object-ID and plan calculations are reused. Conceptual P4-D geometry is not a surveyed or approved physical setup; visibility is not detection accuracy. Saved records contain reasoning/settings, not frozen rendered measurements. |
| S-EXP-03 · fault consequences | Ten independently committed snapshots: ready, interruption, report, classification, restriction, repair, interrupted verification, refused early release, verified, released. Named next owners and projections for retained student evidence, complete kit, faculty rotations, plan and public notice. | Existing ISS-0417 rules run in an independent authored sandbox. No operational dispatch, primary-state write, notification or device action. Assessor tooling does not confer student authority. Public projection excludes issue, person, booking, repair and evidence details. |

Rehearsal entrypoints are `#/E01/rehearsal`, `#/E06/rehearsal` and `#/scene/rehearsal`; the assessor walkthrough is `#/walkthrough`. Preparation, bench, recorded-context and technician links make them discoverable. Retained-context links use the capture's own reference; missing or unusable historical context refuses substitution and offers an explicitly independent fixture rehearsal.

Prediction and reflection remain in memory until explicitly saved. Rehearsals use separate `parallax-rehearsal-v1:E01`, `:E06` and `:E12` records. Save checks the previously read raw value and refuses a competing write. Malformed/unsupported saved data are retained. Fresh start and restore archive raw records before replacement and refuse if a backup cannot be written. Current unsaved results are exported during explicit recovery. Raw and current JSON exports remain available. Reload restores the saved prediction, parameter, source/settings and reflection; it does not restore an unsaved draft.
