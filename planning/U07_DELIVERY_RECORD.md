# U07 delivery — operational depth

3 October 2026 · Complete for the independent local operations exercise and operating reference.

The operations desk at `#/operations` brings complete-kit allocation, request recovery, method applicability, handover acceptance and permitted public consequences into one reviewable workspace. Faculty, technician and booking views link to it. Existing attendance, asset, student and booking workflows remain the primary React example record; this exercise reads their availability and activity without changing them.

The scope follows the blueprint's conditional shared-backend gate. No institutional account, authoritative database, external notification or device integration is connected. Prepared actor selection exercises ownership rules; it is not authentication. A serialised in-memory repository demonstrates the final allocation rules in one browser instance. It does not establish cross-process transactions, durable shared reservations or real staff approval.

## Delivered behavior

- Allocation expands the canonical kit into all required components, including both individual lights, station and capture space. Overlapping intervals are checked inside the final serialised transition; exactly one competing request commits the whole kit. Adjacency, different dates and the independent signal kit are handled separately. A conflict creates no partial hold and can return a checked, unreserved alternative.
- Stable request identity binds exact content and prepared actor. Same-request retry returns the original record without duplicate allocation or audit event. Changed content or actor is refused. Deliberately lost acknowledgement remains unknown until lookup/retry. Explicitly saved results can also be looked up by their original ID after reload.
- Offline drafts keep their original intention, actor and condition versions. Another normal request does not erase them. Reconnection checks current allocations, method publication and equipment restrictions; a taken slot is rejected instead of overwritten. An unsaved draft is not a remote or confirmed booking.
- Faculty withdrawal requires a reason and an explicit treatment of the existing exercise session. New allocations are blocked, the recorded method revision and exact illustration remain, and the next owner is visible. An explicit retained-method decision cannot bypass the technician's camera restriction. Policy dates are prepared metadata; no clock, automatic expiry or institutional approval is simulated. U06 guide publication is independent and unchanged.
- Handover records a fact, correct next owner, due condition and lab-lead absence cover. It remains awaiting acceptance until the named owner accepts it. Public notices require the supporting method/equipment condition and its responsible actor. The projection contains only consequence, subject, source version and exercise identity/revision. Private notes, reasons, requests, bookings and evidence are excluded. Withdrawing a notice preserves history and the underlying restriction.
- Explicit browser snapshots use a separate key, `parallax-operations-exercise-v1`. Reads retain malformed/unsupported text. Saving checks the retained raw version and archives it under a unique key before replacement; an archive failure blocks replacement. Detected competing writes are refused. This is browser recovery, not an atomic shared storage protocol. A fresh in-memory exercise retains the previous ledger, pending draft and request for a reversible restore.
- Exportable JSON is readable in a native disclosure and available through download controls. Pending drafts are included in export but are not automatically replayed or restored on reload. The in-app browser's download event did not establish completed file delivery; the visible JSON and in-memory restore were verified. Browser clearing/quota failure can still remove local persistence.

The printable [operating reference](../apps/lab-web/public/operations-runbook.html) and [Markdown copy](../apps/lab-web/public/operations-runbook.md) are generated from the same eight runbooks and five operating owners used by the interface. Each recovery identifies symptom, preserved record, responsible owner, minimal diagnostics, permitted recovery and verification. Four operating options and nine unpriced cost/effort categories state added responsibilities without invented prices, procurement approval or savings. Real pilots require an authoritative record and reconciliation owner, retention/access decisions, qualified absence cover and backup/recovery verification.
