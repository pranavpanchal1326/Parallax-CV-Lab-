Final Year Project Assignment
UI/UX Design Proposal for State-of-the-Art Engineering Laboratories
Course
UI/UX Design / Human Computer Interaction / Product Design
Assignment Title
Designing User-Centered UI/UX Solutions for Modern Engineering Laboratories
Background
Engineering laboratories are evolving into smart, technology-enabled learning environments that promote innovation, collaboration, safety, and experiential learning. A well-designed laboratory should provide intuitive navigation, effective space utilization, accessibility, safety, digital interaction, and an engaging learning experience.
As a final-year engineering student, you are required to develop a comprehensive UI/UX solution for one of the proposed laboratories in the new Engineering Innovation Centre.
Each laboratory has an available area of 70 m² and should be designed considering modern educational practices, Industry 4.0 concepts, sustainability, accessibility, and smart laboratory management.

Laboratories
Each student/team will be assigned one of the following laboratories:
Embedded Systems Lab (70 m²) 
Digital Electronics Lab (70 m²) 
Semiconductor / VLSI Lab (70 m²) 
Communication Lab (70 m²) 
Product Design Lab (70 m²) 
Signal & Computer Vision Lab (70 m²) 
Basic Electronics Lab (70 m²) 
Basic Electrical Lab (70 m²) 
Texas Instruments Centre of Excellence (70 m²) 
IIT Bombay Robotics Centre of Excellence (70 m²) 

Problem Statement
Design a complete UI/UX solution for the assigned laboratory that enhances student learning, improves usability, ensures safety, and creates an engaging smart laboratory environment.
The design should focus on both physical user experience (space, furniture, movement, accessibility) and digital user experience (interactive displays, dashboards, mobile/web interfaces, booking systems, digital signage, IoT integration, etc.).
Objectives
The proposed solution should:
Improve user experience 
Enhance laboratory safety 
Increase equipment accessibility 
Simplify laboratory management 
Encourage collaborative learning 
Support smart and digital learning 
Follow universal accessibility principles 
Optimize utilization of the available 70 m² space
