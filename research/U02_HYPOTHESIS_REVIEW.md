# PARALLAX — all twelve hypotheses reviewed

U02-R2 · 3 October 2026. All twelve remain **unvalidated local assumptions**; primary research is excluded from the agreed scope. External relevance is not local confirmation. Earlier high/medium confidence labels were author estimates, not collected evidence.

## H01 · Students discover missing or incompatible kit only at the bench.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S04, S06, S07.

Preparation and equipment permissions are meaningful design contexts; none establishes when our students discover problems.

**Desk review:** Inspect kit/preparation dependency coverage; local incidence and timing are unknown.

**Possible counterevidence:** Current manifests and staff checks already prevent missing-kit surprises.

## H02 · Current booking covers room and time, not individual devices and accessories.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S06, S07, S13, S14.

External policies and accounts show several equipment-booking practices; the local system remains unknown.

**Desk review:** Compare external booking practices and the whole-kit concept; local policy remains an assumption.

**Possible counterevidence:** The local system already reserves resources, or station-only booking is appropriate for fixed equipment.

## H03 · Acquisition settings are lost between capture and report writing.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S01, S05.

Reasoning and structured provenance justify traceable records; actual loss and its frequency are not established.

**Desk review:** Trace retained context through capture, comparison, notebook and receipt; local loss frequency is unknown.

**Possible counterevidence:** Current instruments and notebooks retain the settings students need without extra work.

## H04 · Faults are reported verbally and do not reach future bookers.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S06, S13.

Fault ownership and coordination warrant investigation; neither source describes our reporting chain.

**Desk review:** Walk through report ownership, restriction propagation and verified release; local handover practice is unknown.

**Possible counterevidence:** Existing logbooks and staff handovers already communicate faults reliably.

## H05 · Attendance is taken on paper and corrected informally.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption — no direct selected secondary support. **Sources:** No direct selected source / coverage.

No selected source establishes the local attendance method. Preserve the requirement as provisional.

**Desk review:** Inspect correction provenance and mark the absence of direct secondary evidence for local attendance practice.

**Possible counterevidence:** The institution already has a mandatory digital attendance system with an audit trail.

## H06 · Novice students hesitate with unfamiliar instruments and wait for help.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S04, S01, S12.

Preparation and scaffolding are plausible responses; waiting behaviour is a local question, not an observed finding.

**Desk review:** Review staged guidance and support placement; local hesitation/waiting behaviour is unobserved.

**Possible counterevidence:** Pauses are deliberate reasoning, and peer/faculty help is already timely and educationally useful.

## H07 · Faculty cannot see cohort readiness before a session starts.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S02.

Learning expectations merit explicit investigation; E-CLASS does not demonstrate a missing cohort-readiness view.

**Desk review:** Separate readiness definitions from learning outcomes; no local faculty need is validated.

**Possible counterevidence:** Faculty already get sufficient readiness information; aggregate completion would not improve support.

## H08 · Asset location relies on technician memory.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S06, S10.

Equipment responsibility and organisation are relevant contexts; no selected source establishes memory dependence.

**Desk review:** Trace canonical identifiers and movement history; no local memory-dependence finding exists.

**Possible counterevidence:** A maintained register and labels already resolve location accurately.

## H09 · Students already use phones for reference during experiments.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption — no direct selected secondary support. **Sources:** No direct selected source / coverage.

Responsive design is a product commitment; it does not prove phone use in this lab. Earlier confidence is not evidence.

**Desk review:** Inspect responsive access alternatives; device use and phone policy remain unknown.

**Possible counterevidence:** Phones are prohibited, inconvenient or inaccessible; shared bench displays are the usual reference.

## H10 · Circulation and uncontrolled light disturb camera capture.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption — no direct selected secondary support. **Sources:** No direct selected source / coverage.

The assumed geometry and illustrative lighting fixture do not establish real room disturbances.

**Desk review:** Inspect assumed circulation/capture geometry; actual light and movement disturbance are unmeasured.

**Possible counterevidence:** Existing separation and controlled lighting already protect capture; another cause explains failures.

## H11 · A 3D map helps orientation more than it helps booking.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S08.

Accessibility supports equivalent task paths, not a ranking of 3D usefulness.

**Desk review:** Inspect list/plan equivalence to spatial tasks; no comparative user-preference result exists.

**Possible counterevidence:** 3D adds effort or distraction; the list/plan is equally effective for orientation.

## H12 · Students treat model confidence as correctness.

**Status:** Unvalidated local assumption. **Sources:** S02, S03, S11, S12.

Epistemic expectations and AI-learning evidence justify probing interpretation; none directly tests this model-confidence claim.

**Desk review:** Review confidence/correctness boundaries and claims; no local misconception prevalence is established.

**Possible counterevidence:** Students already distinguish model score, calibration and independent ground truth.
