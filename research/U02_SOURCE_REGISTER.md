## S01 · Teaching critical thinking

N. G. Holmes, C. E. Wieman and D. A. Bonn · 2015. **Primary research · historical-cohort comparison. Reading depth: full.**

[Canonical source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.04870) · [Text read](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.04870) · DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1505329112. Locator: Main paper: introduction, instructional approach and results; historical-cohort description.

- **Reported finding:** Introductory physics students practised comparing data and models, choosing improvements and reflecting; prompts were progressively removed. The authors report improved experimental reasoning, including later-course evidence.
- **Limit:** One institution and historical cohorts, not random assignment. Does not establish the effect of this interface or transfer a learning-effect size to PARALLAX.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: compare retained conditions, ask for an evidence-based next investigation and evaluate independent reasoning.

## S02 · An epistemology and expectations survey about experimental physics: Development and initial results

B. M. Zwickl, T. Hirokawa, N. Finkelstein and H. J. Lewandowski · 2014; preprint revised 2 May 2014. **Primary instrument-development research. Reading depth: abstract.**

[Canonical source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1055) · DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevSTPER.10.010120. Locator: Abstract: paired perspectives and initial formative deployment in 45 classes at 20 institutions.

- **Reported finding:** E-CLASS compares students' own views of experimental physics with their perceptions of professional practice and grading expectations.
- **Limit:** Abstract read. This is not a navigation-usability instrument, and PARALLAX has not administered or validated E-CLASS.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: ask separately what students think the experiment teaches and what they believe earns marks.

## S03 · Confirming what we know: Understanding questionable research practices in intro physics labs

Martin M. Stein, Emily M. Smith and N. G. Holmes · 2018 preprint. **Primary qualitative research. Reading depth: full.**

[Canonical source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04716) · [Text read](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.04716v1). Locator: Methods, analysis of student records, results and discussion.

- **Reported finding:** Analysis of records from 107 groups at three institutions connects confirmation-oriented laboratory goals with questionable research practices.
- **Limit:** Context-specific qualitative evidence. It does not establish dishonesty in this cohort or validate H12 about model confidence.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: retain failed attempts and revisions; prompt limitations and alternative explanations rather than a predetermined correct conclusion.

## S05 · Structure-based knowledge acquisition from electronic lab notebooks for research data provenance documentation

Max Schröder, Susanne Staehlke, Paul Groth, J. Barbara Nebe, Sascha Spors and Frank Krüger · 31 January 2022. **Primary provenance proof of concept. Reading depth: abstract.**

[Canonical source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35101121/) · DOI: 10.1186/s13326-021-00257-x. Locator: PubMed abstract and figure captions; PMC full text presented an access challenge.

- **Reported finding:** Structured notebook content is used to acquire provenance information; a biomedical laboratory proof of concept covers seven protocols for the automated approach.
- **Limit:** Abstract and captions read. Not evidence of student usability, complete automatic metadata capture or this app's scientific validity.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: link the executed record to method, source, parameters and revision; distinguish a protocol template from what actually happened.

## S06 · CALM user guidelines and rules

University of Edinburgh · Confocal and Advanced Light Microscopy · Undated live page; accessed 3 October 2026. **Primary institutional operating policy. Reading depth: full.**

[Canonical source](https://clinical-sciences.ed.ac.uk/qmri-facilities/calm-confocal-and-advanced-light-microscopy/user-guidelines). Locator: Training and registration; bookings; data handling; reporting failures; equipment shutdown instructions.

- **Reported finding:** This facility specifies training, equipment booking, fault reporting and data responsibilities. Its laser shutdown instructions depend on the next booking.

## S08 · Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2

W3C · Recommendation; current linked edition accessed 3 October 2026. **Primary normative web standard. Reading depth: full.**

[Canonical source](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). Locator: SC 1.4.10 Reflow, 2.1.1 Keyboard, 2.4.7 Focus Visible, 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), 4.1.3 Status Messages.

- **Reported finding:** The recommendation defines testable web-accessibility criteria, including reflow, keyboard operation and programmatically determinable status messages.
- **Limit:** A standard is not a certificate. These selected criteria are not the entire AA checklist, nor a physical-accessibility assessment.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: provide typed IDs and list alternatives, visible keyboard focus and announced recovery; assess all applicable AA criteria separately.

## S09 · Using CO2 monitors

UK Health and Safety Executive · Live guidance; accessed 3 October 2026. **Primary public guidance. Reading depth: full.**

[Canonical source](https://www.hse.gov.uk/ventilation/using-co2-monitors.htm). Locator: What monitoring can tell you; monitor choice/placement; representative readings and recording.

- **Reported finding:** CO2 monitoring can provide a broad indication of ventilation. Snapshot readings can mislead and cannot demonstrate safe levels.
- **Limit:** UK guidance, not Indian legal compliance or a specification for this unsurveyed room. No numerical alarm threshold is imported.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: show measurement provenance, age and quality; require an approved sampling plan before using observations for operational decisions.

## S11 · Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics

Hamsa Bastani, Osbert Bastani, Alp Sungu, Haosen Ge, Özge Kabakcı and Rei Mariman · 2025; correction notice 20 August 2025. **Primary randomised field research. Reading depth: full-main-correction-unread.**

[Canonical source](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) · DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2422633122. Locator: Abstract, significance and experimental design. Linked correction body could not be retrieved.

- **Reported finding:** The high-school mathematics trial contrasts unrestricted assistance with a teacher-guided tutor. Its reported outcomes distinguish supported practice from later unassisted performance.
- **Limit:** Different age, subject and task. Main article read, linked correction body unavailable; effect-size claims excluded. Does not evaluate a laboratory assistant.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: hint before answering; require a student attempt and evaluate unaided understanding, with faculty review of source and pedagogy.

## S12 · AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting

Greg Kestin, Kelly Miller, Anna Klales, Timothy Milbourne and Gregorio Ponti · 3 June 2025. **Primary randomised crossover research. Reading depth: full.**

[Canonical source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-97652-6) · DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6. Locator: Methods, results and limitations; two undergraduate physics lessons, 194 eligible students.

- **Reported finding:** The authors report strong post-test outcomes from a carefully constructed physics tutor compared with in-class active learning in a two-lesson crossover study.
- **Limit:** At-home versus classroom conditions, short horizon and expert-designed material. No evidence here for equipment operation, long-term retention or replacing faculty.
- **Product implication:** Design inference: evaluate a narrowly scoped, source-linked hint experience as a complement to teaching, rather than assuming a general chatbot helps.
