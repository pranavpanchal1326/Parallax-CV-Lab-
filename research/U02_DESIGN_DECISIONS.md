# PARALLAX — U02 design decision record

U02-R2 · 3 October 2026. These are reasoned design choices, not experimentally validated product findings. Existing implementation is distinguished from future concepts. Sources resolve in [the register](U02_SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

## D-U02-01 · Preserve the student task entry; keep Smart Lab as a distinct overview

**Chosen:** Today → method → kit → experiment; dashboard leads staff to an owned attention task.

**Alternative considered:** Make metrics the universal homepage.

**Rationale / boundary:** Authored hierarchy keeps the learning task legible while satisfying the dashboard deliverable. The sources support reasoning/access, not proof that this navigation is best.

**Sources:** S01, S02, S08. **State:** Implemented direction; not participant validated.

**Review / implementation check:** DR01/DR09: inspect next-action hierarchy and metric definitions; navigation preference remains unmeasured.

## D-U02-02 · Preparation, training permission and reservation are separate

**Chosen:** Show method, versioned quantities, conflict reason and retained alternative; expose permission as a future policy boundary.

**Alternative considered:** Treat a completed read checklist as equipment certification, or automatically book only the room.

**Rationale / boundary:** External facilities supply relevant operating examples. Local booking and training policy still need confirmation; a complex scheduler is not automatically the right product.

**Sources:** S04, S06, S07, S13, S14. **State:** Kit and acknowledgement demonstrated; real permission/policy integration open.

**Review / implementation check:** DR02/DR03/DR04: check dependency and permission boundaries; deployment policy approval remains conditional.

## D-U02-03 · Record before interpreting; compare immutable context

**Chosen:** Retain method/source/settings/time at capture; unknown historical context stays unknown; ask for the student's bounded interpretation.

**Alternative considered:** Reconstruct old context from current settings or optimise for a predetermined correct answer.

**Rationale / boundary:** A traceable record supports the intended scientific experience. Visual or numeric differences alone do not establish causation.

**Sources:** S01, S03, S05. **State:** Local condition replay implemented; full processing/provenance parity open.

**Review / implementation check:** DR05/DR06/DR07: check immutable source/context and bounded interpretation; learning effects remain unmeasured.

## D-U02-04 · Make recovery and responsibility observable

**Chosen:** Distinguish draft/saved/submitted/receipt and physical return; faults retain restrictions until verification and have an owner.

**Alternative considered:** Celebrate success on click or let an incomplete repair silently restore availability.

**Rationale / boundary:** This is a service and state-integrity decision informed by provenance, policy examples and status-message requirements, not evidence of local failure frequency.

**Sources:** S05, S06, S08. **State:** Local recovery and technician sequence demonstrated; authoritative shared service open.

**Review / implementation check:** DR08/DR11/DR15: reconcile request/receipt, restriction/release and handover contracts; real-service authority is separate.

## D-U02-05 · Observations and energy previews remain qualified

**Chosen:** Source/age/quality first; modelled upper bound, protected bookings/essential services and five-part sustainability ledger; human review.

**Alternative considered:** One green Safe score, generic idle auto-off or invented savings.

**Rationale / boundary:** Guidance and operating examples require context-specific interpretation. Every physical decision needs approved measurements and asset policy.

**Sources:** S06, S09, S10. **State:** Simulated observations and energy model implemented; connected hardware/physical approval open.

**Review / implementation check:** DR10: inspect provenance, model assumptions and protection reasons; physical sampling/shutdown policy remains a deployment boundary.

## D-U02-06 · Build AI as narrow help with a visible evidence boundary

**Chosen:** Manual lookup cites an approved revision; reasoning mode requests an attempt and offers a hint; unknown facts trigger abstention/staff route.

**Alternative considered:** General answer chatbot, automatic notebook claims or equipment control.

**Rationale / boundary:** Research supports evaluating pedagogy and unaided understanding rather than assuming all AI assistance helps. These studies do not validate our proposed assistant.

**Sources:** S01, S03, S11, S12. **State:** Concept and evaluation brief only; implementation belongs to U06.

**Review / implementation check:** U06 source-grounding and abstention/error/privacy evaluations; no participant-learning outcome will be claimed.

## D-U02-07 · Access alternatives are first-class paths

**Chosen:** Typed ID, plan/list, keyboard, reflow and explicit status; optional scan/3D/voice/AR cannot be the sole path.

**Alternative considered:** Spatial or camera interaction as the only way to complete a task.

**Rationale / boundary:** Selected WCAG criteria are normative design inputs. They do not confirm full conformance, preferred device or physical circulation.

**Sources:** S08. **State:** Alternatives exist across prototypes; complete applicable-AA audit remains implementation work; participant validation not conducted and excluded.

**Review / implementation check:** DR04/DR12 plus keyboard/reflow/access checks; device preference and full conformance are separate evidence.

## D-U02-08 · Measure adoption and learning separately from completion

**Chosen:** Document policy owner, evidence definition and denominator; collect baseline before utilisation/learning/savings claims.

**Alternative considered:** Dashboard completion as learning, reservations as utilisation or modelled watts as savings.

**Rationale / boundary:** Institutional and public evidence raises organisation and expectation questions. The unspecified old utilisation citation cannot establish a local baseline.

**Sources:** S02, S10, S13, S14. **State:** Research/measurement contract delivered; no baseline or operational analytics result.

**Review / implementation check:** Review definitions, ownership, source gaps and denominators; do not claim local utilisation, learning or savings.

No product-state, source-fixture, geometry or scientific-result changes are made by this research increment. R14–R16 gained dedicated supplementary wireframes because the original twelve-board set did not cover those operational tasks. All three remain implementation/validation work for the relevant upgrade phase.
