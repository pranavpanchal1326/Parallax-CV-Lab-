Honest boundaries

Local scope and final review

What this is

Research is secondary-only, with fourteen reviewed sources and zero local participants. Scientific examples, sensors, energy and room geometry carry their declared model and source boundaries. Ollama reads reviewed public guides and receives no student records.

What remains open

A spoken screen-reader pass, a physical phone and a human presenter rehearsal remain unverified. Team names, contributions and the deadline year were not supplied. Live hardware, shared authenticated operations and site approval remain separate integrations.
